## 5. Customer FAQ baseline

**What is QR-V?** QR-V is a registry-backed verification architecture and software offering that connects QR identifiers to issuer records and verification results.

**Is QR-V just a QR-code generator?** No. The code is a pointer; the intended product verifies canonical record status, issuer and integrity.

**Can QR-V verify certificates?** Certificates are the first commercial use case. A controlled pilot can be scoped through https://qrv.network/contact. Production availability and scope require confirmation.

**How do I confirm a QR-V record?** Use https://qrv.network/verify. Do not rely on an agent's guess or an old screenshot.

**Can I issue a certificate today?** Request issuer onboarding and current provisioning status. Do not promise immediate activation.

**Can this verify government IDs or property title?** QR-V can check registered issuer data within its authorized scope; it does not automatically confer government, licensing, recording-office, notarization, or title authority.

**How much does it cost?** See https://qrv.network/pricing. Plans and implementation packages have distinct pricing, and certain offers require written agreement.

**Do you offer an API?** QR-V documents a verification API; integration access and production availability must be confirmed at https://qrv.network/developers or through the inquiry form.

**Can this chat complete an order or verify my private certificate?** No. Use the corresponding secure company service; never paste secrets or private documents into the agent.

## 6. Catalog/feed gating

The file \`qrv_woocommerce_45_products.csv\` in the project is a **45-row import catalog**, not a verified live inventory. It has product names, descriptions, prices, SKUs and categories but no product URLs, image URLs, current availability, or proof of active fulfillment.

Under OpenAI's stable feed format, a discovery feed requires (at minimum) item_id, title, description, url, brand, seller_name, image_url, availability, price, target_countries and store_country. Until each included item has a real public product URL, direct authorized image URL, accurate USD price, availability, and content compliance review, **do not submit this CSV as a ChatGPT commerce feed**.

Flag titles such as "Fraud-Proof Invoice Verification System" for revision: a QR image can be cloned or replaced, and absolute anti-fraud claims are not supported. Do not describe a template as an officially issued business license or credential.

The Issuer Portal subscription and implementation services should primarily be represented as service offerings within Business Hub, not retrofitted into physical retail inventory. Whether any digital product is supported in the feed must be confirmed with the current format and Business Hub capability.

**Do not enable ChatGPT checkout solely by setting product eligibility flags.** This is a separate capability and not established by this document.
