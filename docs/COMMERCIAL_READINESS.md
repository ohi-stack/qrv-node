# QR-V Commercial Readiness

The Issuer Portal is the primary commercial application. The first governed product is verified certificates, followed by membership verification, product authentication, API access, and white-label deployments.

## Launch-capable workflow

The repository implements the controlled single-issuer certificate lifecycle: login -> issue -> QRVID -> downloadable QR -> public verify -> revoke -> public REVOKED result. Verified Contact Cards add stable QRVID updates, field-level disclosure, VCF download, and aggregate analytics.

## Commercial gates still required

- approved issuer application and due-diligence procedure;
- enforceable issuer terms, acceptable-use policy, privacy notice, data-processing terms, support policy, and service-level terms;
- subscription provider, signed webhook handling, plan catalog, entitlements, invoices, taxes, refunds, cancellation, and failed-payment behavior;
- multi-user team RBAC and recovery-safe identity provider;
- per-issuer API credentials, rotation, revocation, usage metering, quotas, and developer onboarding;
- white-label tenant isolation, domain verification, certificate automation, and brand controls;
- audit exports and customer evidence reports;
- support ticket provider and incident communications;
- three live governed demos: certificate, membership, and product.

Pricing displayed in the interface is a product proposal until billing, entitlements, and legal terms are approved and connected. No placeholder route may be represented as an operational paid feature.
